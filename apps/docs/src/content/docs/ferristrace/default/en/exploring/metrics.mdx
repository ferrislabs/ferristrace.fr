# Metrics

The **Metrics** explorer (`/metrics`, shortcut `g` then `m`) browses every OpenTelemetry metric ingested into your organisation and charts aggregated time series with exemplar links.

## Metric catalogue

When you open `/metrics`, FerrisTrace lists the discovered metric catalogue (`GET /api/orgs/{org}/metrics`) with each metric's:

- **Name** (for example `http.server.request.duration`, `system.cpu.utilization`, `db.client.connections.usage`)
- **Kind** (`gauge`, `sum`, or `histogram`)
- **Unit** (UCUM unit reported by the instrument, such as `ms`, `s`, `By`, `1`)
- **Description** and **last seen** timestamp

## Querying and grouping series

Select a metric to build a chart (`GET` or `POST /api/orgs/{org}/metrics/{name}/series`):

- **Aggregations** — Choose temporal and spatial aggregations suited to the instrument kind: `avg`, `sum`, `min`, `max`, `rate`, `increase`, and histogram percentiles (`p50`, `p90`, `p95`, `p99`).
- **Attribute filters** — Filter by `service` or any metric/resource attribute discovered for that metric (`/api/orgs/{org}/metrics/{name}/attributes` and `.../values`), plus Kubernetes placement (`cluster`, `namespace`, `workload`, `pod`, `node`).
- **Group by** — Split series by one or more label keys (for example `http.response.status_code`, `k8s.pod.name`, or `host.name`).

## Jumping from exemplars to traces

When your OpenTelemetry SDK records **exemplars** on counters or histograms (enabled automatically by official SDKs when sampling traces alongside metrics), FerrisTrace stores them in `metric_exemplars` with their `trace_id`, `span_id`, timestamp, and value.

Exemplar points are plotted directly on the metric chart: clicking an exemplar opens the exact slow or failing trace waterfall in **Traces** so you never have to guess which request caused a p99 spike.

## Adding charts to incidents and dashboards

- **Add to dashboard** — Save any metric query directly into a new or existing [Dashboard](/en/ferristrace/exploring/dashboards).
- **Attach to incident** — Pin the metric chart and active window to an open incident's timeline as permanent evidence.
